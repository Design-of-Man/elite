Meet the Doctor

Marc F. Matarazzo, MD

Board-certified and fellowship-trained, with over 23 years specializing in sports medicine and minimally invasive arthroscopic surgery. A Fellow of the American Academy of Orthopaedic Surgeons and active member of the American Orthopaedic Society for Sports Medicine, Dr. Matarazzo is certified in the MAKO robotic-assisted knee replacement system and was the first surgeon in Florida to implant a MISHA knee implant.

  • Fellowship-trained at Lenox Hill Hospital, NYC — former Assistant Team Physician, NY Jets & NY Islanders
  • MD, Lewis Katz School of Medicine at Temple University
  • Certified, MAKO robotic-assisted total & partial knee replacement
