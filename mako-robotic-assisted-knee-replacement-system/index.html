MAKO Robotic-Assisted Knee Replacement

By Marc F. Matarazzo, MD · November 27, 2024

The MAKO Robotic-Assisted Knee Replacement System is an advanced technology designed to provide greater precision, improved outcomes, and faster recovery for patients undergoing knee replacement surgery. The system uses 3D imaging and robotic assistance to build a surgical plan tailored to each patient's unique anatomy.

How It Works

Personalized planning — before surgery, a detailed 3D model of your knee is created to map the exact areas that need treatment.

Precision surgery — during the procedure, the surgeon uses the MAKO robotic arm to assist in removing damaged bone and cartilage while preserving as much healthy tissue as possible.

Optimal implant placement — the robotic system ensures precise placement of the knee implant, improving alignment and stability for a more natural feel.

Benefits of MAKO Robotic Knee Replacement

  • Greater accuracy — optimal implant positioning for improved function and longevity
  • Minimally invasive — smaller incisions lead to less pain, reduced scarring, and faster healing
  • Faster recovery — quicker rehabilitation and a smoother return to daily activity
  • Customized approach — surgery tailored to your knee's unique structure

If you're considering knee replacement surgery, the MAKO Robotic-Assisted System may offer a more precise, less invasive solution with a quicker recovery time.
