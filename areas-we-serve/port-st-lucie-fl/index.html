What Dr. Matarazzo treats at this office

Dr. Marc F. Matarazzo is board-certified and fellowship-trained, with more than 23 years treating professional athletes and everyday patients. He trained at Lenox Hill Hospital in New York and served as an Assistant Team Physician for the New York Jets and New York Islanders. He is certified in the MAKO robotic-assisted system and was the first surgeon in Florida to implant a MISHA knee implant.

  • ACL reconstruction
  • Meniscus repair
  • MAKO robotic-assisted knee replacement
  • Total shoulder replacement
  • Cartilage restoration and MACI
  • Orthobiologics and regenerative therapies
  • Shockwave therapy
  • Sports medicine
  • Second opinions
  • Workers’ compensation injuries
