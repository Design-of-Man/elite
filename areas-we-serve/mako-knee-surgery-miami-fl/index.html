FAQ for Miami Patients

MAKO knee surgery questions, answered

MAKO is an advanced robotic-assisted technology that helps surgeons create a personalized surgical plan and perform knee replacement with enhanced precision based on your unique anatomy.

You may be a candidate if you suffer from severe knee arthritis, chronic knee pain, stiffness, or reduced mobility that has not improved with physical therapy, medications, injections, or other conservative treatments.

Potential benefits include personalized surgical planning, precise implant positioning, improved joint alignment, protection of healthy bone and tissue, and a quicker return to daily activities.

Recovery varies for every patient, but many begin walking shortly after surgery and continue improving through physical therapy over the following weeks and months.

Scheduling a consultation is the first step. During your visit, Dr. Marc Matarazzo will evaluate your condition, review imaging, discuss your treatment options, and determine whether MAKO robotic-assisted knee replacement is right for you.
