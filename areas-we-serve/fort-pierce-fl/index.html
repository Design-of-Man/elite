Knee Pain

Knee pain treatment for Fort Pierce patients

Each visit starts the same way: an exam and a review of any imaging with Dr. Matarazzo. Surgery is one option among several, alongside non-surgical treatment, and he will tell you plainly if waiting is the better course.

  • Knee ligament injuries — ACL, PCL, MCL and LCL reconstruction, and meniscus repair
  • Knee arthritis — total knee replacement, partial knee replacement and MAKO robotic-assisted knee replacement, for which Dr. Matarazzo is certified
  • The MISHA Knee System — Dr. Matarazzo was the first surgeon in Florida to implant it
  • Non-surgical options — PRP injections, other orthobiologics and shockwave therapy

Shoulder Pain

Shoulder pain and rotator cuff tears

An ache at night, pain reaching overhead and weakness lifting the arm usually point to the rotator cuff. Many partial and age-related tears settle with rehabilitation; full-thickness tears in active people, and tears with sudden weakness after an injury, are worth assessing early, because a torn tendon left alone can pull back and become harder to repair.

  • Rotator cuff repair — arthroscopic wherever the tear allows
  • Shoulder instability and dislocation
  • Total shoulder replacement and reverse shoulder replacement for advanced arthritis and irreparable tears

More on the signs in shoulder pain that won’t go away.

Sports & Work Injuries

Sports injuries, work injuries and accidents

Dr. Matarazzo spent over a decade as a team physician for the NY Jets and NY Islanders, and he treats sports injuries at every level, from competitive athletes to active adults. Treatment may include rehabilitation, activity modification, regenerative therapies and, when necessary, minimally invasive surgery.

  • Sports medicine — tendon injuries, muscle strains, stress fractures and sprains
  • ACL and other ligament injuries
  • Work injuries and car-accident and fall injuries, with the documentation a claim requires
  • Second opinions on a surgery or treatment plan recommended elsewhere
