FAQ for Naples Patients

MAKO knee surgery questions, answered

Many patients from Naples choose Elite Sports Medicine for advanced robotic-assisted knee replacement, personalized treatment plans, and the expertise of Dr. Marc Matarazzo, a board-certified, fellowship-trained orthopedic surgeon certified in the MAKO robotic-assisted knee replacement system.

MAKO uses 3D imaging and personalized surgical planning to help surgeons perform knee replacement with greater precision and implant alignment based on each patient's unique anatomy.

You may be a candidate if you suffer from severe knee arthritis, chronic knee pain, joint stiffness, swelling, or mobility limitations that have not improved with physical therapy, medications, injections, or other conservative treatments.

Most patients begin walking shortly after surgery, with significant improvements in mobility over the first 6 to 12 weeks. Recovery timelines vary depending on your condition, overall health, and rehabilitation progress.

Many Naples patients choose MAKO knee replacement so they can return to walking, golf, travel, and other low-impact recreational activities after completing recovery and rehabilitation.
