Please confirm:

  • Cleveland Clinic grade 2 and grade 3 LCL timelines are both 8 to 12 weeks as stated on its page; grade 3 may need surgery. Verify you are comfortable publishing these.

The percentages were deliberately not put in patient text; add them if you want. 'Weak leg muscles or prior knee injury' removed from causes (no fetched source). LCL companion injury now worded as Cleveland Clinic states it (ACL tear or other knee problem).
