Appointments & Getting Started

In most cases, no referral is required to schedule a consultation. Some insurance plans require a referral for specialist visits to be covered — check with your insurance provider, or call our office at 561-202-8886 and our staff will help confirm your plan’s requirements before your visit.

We offer same-week appointments in most cases, and Concierge Members receive priority scheduling with direct access to Dr. Matarazzo’s office. Call 561-202-8886 or use the Schedule Appointment page — our team responds the same business day.

Bring a photo ID, your insurance card, a list of current medications, and any relevant imaging (X-rays, MRIs) or records from prior treatment for your injury. If you’ve had imaging done elsewhere, ask that office to send the actual images, not just the report.

Both locations offer the full range of consultation services with Dr. Matarazzo. Choose whichever is closer to you, or call our office and we’ll help you pick based on appointment availability.

Insurance & Cost

Surgery & Recovery

Surgical reconstruction is typically recommended when non-surgical options aren’t enough to restore stability and function, especially for active patients.

MAKO is a robotic-arm assisted surgical system used for total and partial knee replacement and total shoulder replacement. It allows for more precise bone preparation and implant placement than traditional manual technique, which can support a more natural-feeling joint and a smoother recovery. Dr. Matarazzo is certified in the MAKO system.

Recovery timelines vary by patient and activity goals, but most patients follow a structured rehabilitation protocol over several months before returning to full athletic activity. Dr. Matarazzo builds an individualized, sport-specific rehab plan for every ACL reconstruction patient — ask about your specific timeline during your consultation.

Peptide Therapy & Regenerative Medicine

Peptide therapy uses specific, physician-guided compounds (such as BPC-157, TB-500, Ipamorelin, and others) intended to support tissue healing, recovery, and overall wellness. As with any treatment, it should only be pursued under medical supervision — Dr. Matarazzo evaluates whether peptide therapy is appropriate for your specific health profile and goals before prescribing a protocol.

Orthobiologics (such as PRP and other regenerative orthopedic therapies) use substances derived from your own body to support healing at an injury site. Peptide therapy uses specific peptide compounds, often as part of a broader wellness or recovery plan. Dr. Matarazzo can explain which — or which combination — fits your situation during a consultation.
