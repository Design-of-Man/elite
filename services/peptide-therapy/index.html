About Peptide Therapy

Peptide Therapy uses naturally occurring amino-acid chains (peptides) that signal and support specific cellular functions within the body. These peptides help enhance healing, restore tissue balance, regulate inflammation, boost metabolism, and support overall performance and recovery.

This treatment is commonly used in sports medicine to improve tendon and ligament healing, support joint pain treatment, optimize muscle growth and repair, and support hormonal and immune balance.

Because peptides target specific cellular pathways, Elite Sports Medicine personalizes peptide therapy based on your goals, whether that’s relieving joint pain, improving athletic performance, speeding injury recovery, or enhancing vitality.

Peptide therapy is administered through peptide injections, topical creams, nasal sprays, or oral capsules, depending on the specific peptide and therapeutic goal. Protocols Dr. Matarazzo may discuss with you include BPC-157, TB-500, Tesamorelin, Ipamorelin, GHK-Cu, KPV, MOTS-c, CJC-1295 and PT-141.
