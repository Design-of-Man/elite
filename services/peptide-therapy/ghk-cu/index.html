About GHK-Cu in Regenerative & Orthopedic Care

At ELITE Sports Medicine, GHK-Cu therapy may be incorporated into comprehensive regenerative treatment plans designed to support tissue healing, collagen production, and inflammatory regulation. GHK-Cu (Glycyl-L-Histidyl-L-Lysine Copper) is a naturally occurring copper-binding peptide found in human plasma, saliva, and urine. It has been studied for its role in tissue remodeling, wound repair, and cellular regeneration. In orthopedic and sports medicine applications, GHK-Cu may be considered to support: Soft tissue repair, Collagen synthesis, Joint and connective tissue health, Post-injury tissue remodeling, Inflammatory modulation. Treatment is individualized and provided under physician supervision as part of a broader rehabilitation strategy.

Benefits of GHK-Cu Therapy

  • Supports collagen production
  • Encourages tissue remodeling
  • May assist in inflammatory balance
  • Promotes regenerative signaling pathways
  • Complements physical rehabilitation programs
  • Minimally invasive therapeutic option
  • Customized physician-guided protocols

If you are experiencing chronic soft tissue discomfort or delayed recovery, our team can evaluate whether GHK-Cu therapy may be appropriate as part of your personalized care plan.

HOW GHK-Cu MAY WORK

  • Research suggests GHK-Cu may:
  • Stimulate collagen and extracellular matrix production
  • Support wound healing processes
  • Modulate inflammatory responses
  • Influence gene expression related to tissue repair

These mechanisms have generated interest in regenerative medicine and orthopedic applications, though research is ongoing.
