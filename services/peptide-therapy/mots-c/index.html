About MOTS-c Peptide Therapy in Regenerative & Performance Medicine

At ELITE Sports Medicine, MOTS-c peptide therapy may be incorporated into personalized regenerative treatment plans focused on cellular energy regulation, metabolic optimization, recovery support, and performance enhancement. MOTS-c is a mitochondria-derived peptide involved in cellular energy regulation and metabolic processes. Mitochondria play a central role in how the body produces and utilizes energy, particularly in muscle tissue. Emerging research has explored the potential role of MOTS-c in metabolic balance, insulin sensitivity, exercise performance, and cellular adaptation. In orthopedic and sports medicine settings, MOTS-c may be considered to support: cellular energy production, recovery from physical stress, metabolic efficiency, muscle endurance support, and age-related metabolic changes. Treatment is individualized and administered under physician supervision as part of a comprehensive performance and recovery strategy.
