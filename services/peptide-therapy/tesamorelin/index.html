About Tesamorelin Therapy in Regenerative & Performance Medicine

At ELITE Sports Medicine, Tesamorelin therapy may be incorporated into personalized treatment programs focused on recovery optimization, metabolic support, and body composition management. Tesamorelin is a synthetic analog of growth hormone–releasing hormone (GHRH). It works by stimulating the body’s natural production of growth hormone, which plays a role in tissue repair, metabolism, and body composition regulation. In orthopedic and sports medicine settings, Tesamorelin may be considered to support: Recovery after musculoskeletal injury, Lean muscle maintenance, Reduction of excess visceral fat, Metabolic optimization, Age-related decline in growth hormone levels. Treatment is customized and provided under medical supervision as part of a broader performance and regenerative strategy.

How Tesamorelin Therapy May Work

Tesamorelin functions by stimulating the pituitary gland to increase growth hormone release. Growth hormone influences:

Protein synthesis

  • Muscle repair and regeneration
  • Fat metabolism
  • Cellular recovery processes

By supporting natural hormonal signaling pathways, Tesamorelin may assist in optimizing recovery and metabolic balance when clinically indicated.
