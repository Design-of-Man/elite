About PT-141 Therapy for Sexual Health & Wellness

At ELITE Sports Medicine, PT-141 therapy is offered as part of an individualized peptide therapy approach designed to support sexual health, wellness goals, and overall quality of life. PT-141 (Bremelanotide) is a synthetic peptide that activates melanocortin receptors in the central nervous system. Unlike medications that primarily work through blood flow pathways, PT-141 influences neurological pathways involved in sexual arousal and desire. In clinical settings, PT-141 has been studied for its role in addressing certain forms of sexual dysfunction. Within a wellness and performance setting, it may be considered for concerns such as reduced libido, sexual performance concerns, and overall quality-of-life optimization. Treatment is customized and administered under physician supervision following a comprehensive medical evaluation.

  • Individualized physician-guided dosing

If you are experiencing changes in sexual performance or desire, our team can evaluate whether PT-141 therapy may be appropriate for your personalized care plan.

HOW PT-141 Therapy MAY WORK

PT-141 works by activating melanocortin receptors in the brain, which are involved in regulating sexual arousal pathways. Unlike PDE5 inhibitors that primarily increase blood flow, PT-141 influences central nervous system signaling.

This alternative mechanism has generated clinical interest for individuals who may not respond to traditional vascular-based therapies.
