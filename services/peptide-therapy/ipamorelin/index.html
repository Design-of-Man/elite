About Ipamorelin Therapy in Sports & Regenerative Medicine

At ELITE Sports Medicine, Ipamorelin therapy is offered as part of individualized treatment plans focused on recovery enhancement, tissue repair support, and performance optimization. Ipamorelin is a selective growth hormone secretagogue that stimulates the body’s natural production of growth hormone by acting on ghrelin receptors in the pituitary gland. Unlike some other growth hormone–stimulating agents, Ipamorelin is known for its receptor selectivity and targeted mechanism of action. In orthopedic and sports medicine , Ipamorelin may be considered as an adjunct therapy to support the body’s natural healing response in: Soft tissue repair, Muscle recovery following injury, Post-surgical healing support, Age-related recovery decline, Lean muscle maintenance. All treatment plans are individualized and administered under physician supervision as part of a comprehensive recovery program.

HOW IPAMORELIN MAY WORK

Ipamorelin acts by stimulating growth hormone release through activation of specific receptors in the pituitary gland. Growth hormone plays a role in:

Protein synthesis

  • Collagen production
  • Muscle repair
  • Cellular regeneration
  • Metabolic regulation

By supporting natural hormonal pathways, Ipamorelin may assist the body’s recovery and repair mechanisms when medically appropriate.
