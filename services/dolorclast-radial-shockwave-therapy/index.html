  • About DOLORCLAST® Radial Shockwave Therapy

DOLORCLAST® Radial Shockwave Therapy uses high-frequency acoustic pressure waves to stimulate biologic repair in chronically injured tendons, ligaments, fascia, and soft tissue. At ELITE Sports Medicine , this advanced treatment helps promote soft tissue repair , reduce chronic tendon pain, and provide non-surgical pain relief by breaking the chronic pain cycle and restoring function without surgery, injections, or downtime.

Clinically shown to:

  • Reduce chronic tendon pain
  • Improve blood flow and cellular metabolism
  • Break down calcifications and adhesions
  • Stimulate collagen regeneration
  • Improve mobility and function
  • Accelerate return to activity
  • TYPICAL TREATMENT PLAN
  • Based on orthopedic shockwave protocols
  • Standard course
  • 3–6 sessions
  • 1 session per week (strongest evidence-based interval)
  • Each session
  • 5–10 minutes

No downtime. Light activity is permitted; avoid high-impact exercise for 48 hours.

  • How DOLORCLAST® Radial Shockwave Therapy Works
  • Three Key Mechanisms

Mechanical Stimulation - tendon pain Acoustic waves create micro-stress that stimulates fibroblasts and tendon regeneration.

Neovascularization - Increases blood flow, oxygen delivery, and metabolic activity.

Analgesic Effect - Interrupts pain receptor activity and normalizes nerve sensitivity.

What Patients Feel

A firm tapping sensation that is intense but tolerable. Pain decreases significantly as healing progresses—often within the first few sessions.

Benefits of DOLORCLAST® Radial Shockwave Therapy

Ideal for orthopedic and sports medicine patients with: Chronic tendinopathy (Achilles, patellar, gluteal, hamstring, rotator cuff)   Plantar fasciitis & heel pain   Medial or lateral epicondylitis (tennis/golfer’s elbow)   Greater trochanteric pain syndrome   Calcific tendonitis   Trigger points & myofascial pain   Shin splints   Chronic soft-tissue adhesions   Post-injury scar tissue or tightness
