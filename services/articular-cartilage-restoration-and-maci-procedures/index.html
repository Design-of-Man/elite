Articular Cartilage Restoration & MACI Procedure

Articular cartilage restoration and MACI procedures to repair joint surfaces and delay arthritis. Orthopedic care in Port St. Lucie & Palm Beach Gardens.

Schedule a Consultation 561-202-8886

Why doesn’t cartilage heal on its own?

Because it has almost no blood supply. Nearly every other tissue in the body repairs itself through circulation; cartilage does not get that, so a defect tends to stay a defect — and then spread, taking pain, swelling and eventually arthritis with it. Restoration means giving the joint a surface again. That might be microfracture, an osteochondral graft, or MACI, where your own cartilage cells are taken, grown in a laboratory over several weeks, and then implanted on a membrane into the defect. Which one suits you depends on how large the defect is, how much arthritis is already present, how your leg is aligned, and what you need the knee to do afterwards — which is why it starts with an MRI and a proper mapping of the damage. The rehabilitation is deliberately patient. New cartilage has to be loaded gradually or it will not survive.

What we treat

Focal Articular Cartilage Defects

A single defined area of damage, causing pain and catching.

Chondral Flap or Delamination Injury

Cartilage lifting or peeling away from the bone beneath it.

Osteochondral Defects (OCD Lesions)

Damage running through cartilage into the bone below — common in active patients.

Cartilage Wear from Previous Knee Trauma

Wear traceable to an old injury or a knee that was unstable for years.

Cartilage Damage Associated with ACL or Meniscus Injury

Surface damage that arrived alongside an ACL or meniscus injury.

Post-Microfracture Cartilage Failure

Symptoms that returned after an earlier microfracture.

Patellofemoral Cartilage Loss

Loss behind the kneecap, felt as pain at the front of the knee.

Trochlear Groove Cartilage Damage

Damage in the groove the kneecap runs through, which upsets its tracking.

What restoration is for

  • A joint surface to move on again
  • Less pain, swelling and irritation when you use the knee
  • Your own knee kept working for longer
  • The slide toward arthritis slowed
  • Load spread properly across the joint instead of concentrating
  • Movement back, including higher-demand activity
  • With MACI, new cartilage grown from your own cells
  • Rehabilitation written to protect the repair while it takes
  • A return-to-sport progression grounded in evidence
  • Natural joint mechanics restored, and built to last
