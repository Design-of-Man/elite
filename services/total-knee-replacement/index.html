Total Knee Replacement

Full knee joint replacement for advanced arthritis, planned around your anatomy. Board-certified orthopedic surgery in South Florida.

Schedule a Consultation 561-202-8886

When is it time to replace the knee?

Usually the answer arrives through sleep. Pain that stops you walking far, or climbing stairs without planning it, is one thing — pain that wakes you at two in the morning is generally the point at which people decide. The operation resurfaces the joint: the worn cartilage and damaged surfaces come out, and implants designed to reproduce the knee’s natural movement go in. It is the right answer once therapy, medication and injections have stopped buying you meaningful time, and not usually before. What decides how well it goes is alignment, choosing an implant that suits your build and demands, and doing the rehabilitation properly afterwards.

What leads people here?

Severe Knee Osteoarthritis

Cartilage worn through, leaving pain and a joint that will not loosen up.

Post-Traumatic Knee Arthritis

Arthritis that followed an old injury, sometimes decades later.

Degenerative Joint Disease

The slow erosion of the joint surfaces over years of use.

Chronic Knee Pain Unresponsive to Treatment

Pain that has outlasted therapy and injections.

Pain Interfering With Sleep or Rest

An inflamed joint that will not let you sleep.

Knee Swelling and Stiffness

Swelling and stiffness that have quietly taken your range of movement.

Loss of Joint Function Affecting Mobility

A knee that has started deciding what you do with your day.

Failure of Previous Knee Procedures

Earlier arthroscopy or partial procedures that have run out of road.

What the operation is for

  • The chronic pain goes, or drops to something you barely notice
  • Alignment corrected and the leg stable under you again
  • Walking and stairs stop being a calculation
  • Implants built to keep performing for the long run
  • Planning done around your anatomy, not a standard template
  • A structured rehabilitation programme, because that is what decides the result
  • A safe route back to the things you actually want to do
  • Less inflammation, and mechanics that work with you
  • Bowing or angulation corrected, legs back to level
  • Confidence in the knee — which is most of what people actually lost

Come in, get a straight answer about what is wrong, and leave knowing what happens next.
