About MLS Multiwave Laser Therapy

MLS (Multiwave Locked System) Laser Therapy uses synchronized dual wavelengths (808nm + 905nm) to produce deep anti-inflammatory, analgesic, and accelerated tissue-repair effects. At ELITE Sports Medicine , unlike traditional low-level lasers, MLS delivers simultaneous continuous and pulsed emission, creating a more potent and consistent clinical response.

Clinically shown to:

  • Reduce inflammation
  • Decrease pain
  • Improve circulation
  • Accelerate soft tissue healing
  • Enhance postoperative recovery
  • Reduce reliance on NSAIDs or opioids
