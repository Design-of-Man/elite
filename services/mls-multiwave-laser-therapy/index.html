MLS Multiwave Laser Therapy

Schedule a Consultation

Deep tissue repair without a needle or an incision

What is MLS laser therapy?

MLS stands for Multiwave Locked System, and the “locked” part is what distinguishes it. Two wavelengths, 808nm and 905nm, are fired in synchrony rather than one after the other — one working at depth on inflammation and repair, the other cutting pain transmission quickly. Older low-level lasers emit continuously or in pulses. MLS does both at once, which is why the response tends to be stronger and more consistent than earlier generations of the technology.

Clinically shown to:

  • Reduce inflammation
  • Ease pain
  • Improve circulation to the area
  • Speed soft-tissue healing
  • Support recovery after surgery
  • Reduce how much anti-inflammatory or opioid medication is needed

What a course looks like

Built on established orthopedic laser protocols.

  • Acute injuries — six to eight sessions
  • Chronic injuries — ten to twelve sessions
  • After surgery — six to ten sessions
  • Each session — around ten minutes
  • Downtime — none, and no restrictions afterwards

How does MLS work?

  • Two wavelengths, two jobs
  • 808nm reaches deep, working on inflammation and stimulating the tissue
  • 905nm acts fast on pain by interrupting how it is transmitted
  • Firing them in synchrony improves both penetration and consistency
  • What it feels like

Almost nothing — no heat, no vibration, no discomfort. The pain simply recedes across a course of sessions.

Who is it for?

It suits orthopedic and sports patients dealing with:

  • Tendonitis — rotator cuff, patellar or Achilles
  • Bursitis
  • Arthritis in the knee, shoulder, hip or spine
  • Muscle strains and ligament sprains
  • Plantar fasciitis
  • Nerve problems such as neuropathy and neuritis
  • Swelling and pain after surgery
  • Chronic overuse injuries
  • Soft-tissue injuries that will not heal
