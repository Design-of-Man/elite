About Personal Injury Orthopedic Care

When an accident isn't your fault, the last thing you should have to worry about is whether your medical care is documented well enough to support your claim. At ELITE Sports Medicine, board-certified orthopedic surgeon Dr. Marc Matarazzo and our team evaluate and treat orthopedic injuries from car accidents, slip and falls, and other personal injury incidents — while keeping thorough, objective records your attorney and insurer can rely on. We coordinate directly with your attorney's office and any nurse case manager on the file as your case moves forward, and can work with patients on a Letter of Protection basis in many cases so treatment isn't delayed while a claim is pending. As with any orthopedic evaluation here, we start with the least invasive option that will actually work — surgery is reserved for injuries that genuinely need it.

Common Personal Injury Cases We Treat

Motor Vehicle Accident Injuries

Whiplash, soft tissue damage, joint injuries, and fractures resulting from car, motorcycle, or pedestrian collisions.

Slip, Trip, and Fall Injuries

Acute and long-term orthopedic consequences from falls in stores, workplaces, or other public and private property.

Premises Liability Injuries

Injuries resulting from unsafe conditions on someone else's property, including falling objects and defective equipment.

Sports and Recreational Accident Injuries

Ligament tears, tendon injuries, and fractures from accidents during recreational or organized activity.

Soft Tissue and Ligament Injuries

Assessment and treatment of sprains, strains, tears, and movement limitations following an accident.

Fractures and Joint Trauma

Evaluation and, when needed, surgical or non-surgical treatment of broken bones and joint injuries.

Delayed-Onset Pain After an Accident

Evaluation of pain or limited mobility that develops in the days or weeks following an accident.
