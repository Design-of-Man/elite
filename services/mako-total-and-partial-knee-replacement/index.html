About Mako Robotic Total & Partial Knee Replacement

The MAKO™ Robotic-Assisted Knee Replacement system provides a highly precise, personalized approach to knee surgery. This advanced technology allows us to create a 3D model of your knee, assess the exact areas of joint damage, and guide implant placement with exceptional accuracy.By customizing the surgery to your unique anatomy and movement patterns, MAKO helps ensure a more natural-feeling joint, improved alignment, and reduced trauma to surrounding tissue.This method can be used for both Total Knee Replacement and Partial Knee Replacement, depending on the level of joint damage. Patients often experience less pain after surgery, a quicker recovery, and more reliable long-term function compared to traditional methods.This approach is ideal for individuals who want to maintain activity, protect healthy joint structures, and achieve the most personalized surgical outcome possible.

  • Common Conditions Treated with Mako Robotic Total & Partial Knee Replacement
  • Medial Compartment Osteoarthritis

Cartilage wear on the inside of the knee.

Lateral Compartment Osteoarthritis

Cartilage damage on the outside portion of the knee.

Combined/Advanced Joint Degeneration

Multisurface arthritis requiring complete joint replacement.

Chronic Pain with Weight-Bearing Motion

Pain while walking or climbing stairs.

Limited Mobility Affecting Lifestyle

Difficulty performing work or exercise.

Loss of Functional Knee Movement

Reduced mobility affecting daily activity.

Joint Deformity from Long-Term Wear

Bowed or angled leg alignment due to arthritis.

Failure of Conservative Treatments

Pain unresponsive to therapy, injections, or bracing.

Benefits of Mako Robotic Total & Partial Knee Replacement

  • Improved implant alignment and placement accuracy
  • Preserves healthy bone and soft tissue when possible
  • Reduces surgical trauma and promotes faster recovery
  • Provides a more natural-feeling knee movement post-surgery
  • 3D pre-surgical planning tailored to individual anatomy
  • Greater stability and joint balance during motion
  • Lower risk of implant wear over time
  • Ideal for both partial and total joint replacement candidates
  • Helps restore confidence in walking, standing, and daily mobility
  • Supports long-term joint function and overall lifestyle quality
