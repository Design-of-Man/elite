Partial Knee Replacement

When arthritis has worn out only one part of the knee, a partial replacement resurfaces that part and keeps the rest of your own joint. Planned with the MAKO robotic arm, in Port St. Lucie and Palm Beach Gardens.

Schedule a Consultation 561-202-8886

What is a partial knee replacement?

The knee has three compartments: the inner (medial) side, the outer (lateral) side, and the joint behind the kneecap. Arthritis does not always wear them evenly. When the damage is confined to one compartment, a partial knee replacement resurfaces that compartment and leaves the rest of the knee alone, including the cruciate ligaments.

Because less is removed, a partial knee tends to feel more like your own knee, and recovery is usually quicker than after a total replacement. The trade-off is that fewer people qualify, and if arthritis later spreads to the rest of the knee, a second operation may be needed.

Who is a candidate?

Arthritis in one compartment

Most often the inner (medial) side, confirmed on X-ray and examination.

Pain you can point to

Pain that sits on one side of the knee rather than all the way around it.

Ligaments that still work

A knee that is stable, with the ligaments, including the ACL, still doing their job.

Conservative care has run its course

Therapy, injections, bracing and activity changes no longer keep the pain under control.

If the wear has spread across more than one compartment, a total knee replacement is the better operation, and Dr. Matarazzo will tell you so.

How the MAKO robotic arm is used

Dr. Matarazzo is MAKO-certified and performs MAKO partial knee replacements. Before surgery, the MAKO system builds a 3D model of your knee from imaging, showing where the cartilage has worn and where the implant should sit. During the operation the arm holds him to that plan. The robot does not operate; the surgeon does.

With only one compartment being resurfaced and the ligaments left in place, implant position is a large part of the job. That is the part the robotic arm helps with. More on MAKO knee replacement.

Partial, total, or neither?

MISHA Knee System

For early to moderate arthritis on the inner side. Nothing is removed, and a replacement stays available later. About MISHA.

Partial knee replacement

For arthritis confined to one compartment. That compartment is resurfaced and more of your own knee is kept.

Total knee replacement

For wear across the joint. The whole joint surface is replaced.

The partial vs. total knee replacement article compares the two in more detail, and the knee arthritis page covers the non-surgical options that come first.

What to expect

  • An exam and imaging review to see how many compartments are worn
  • A 3D plan of your knee, built before the day of surgery
  • Dr. Matarazzo performing the operation, with the robotic arm holding to the plan
  • Walking with support, for most people, on the day of surgery
  • A structured rehabilitation plan, because that is what decides the result
  • Follow-up at whichever office is closer to you
