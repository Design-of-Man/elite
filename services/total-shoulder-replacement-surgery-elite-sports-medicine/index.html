About Total Shoulder Replacement Surgery

Total Shoulder Replacement Surgery is a highly effective procedure used to relieve pain and restore function in shoulders affected by arthritis, injury, or joint degeneration. During the procedure, the damaged portions of the shoulder joint are replaced with smooth, biocompatible implants designed to restore natural movement and long-term stability.

This procedure is typically recommended when chronic shoulder pain interferes with everyday activities, sleep, lifting, or reaching overhead, and when conservative treatments such as physical therapy, injections, or medications no longer provide lasting relief.

At Elite Sports Medicine , our approach focuses on restoring smooth joint movement, improving shoulder mechanics, and supporting healthy muscle function. Our goal is to help patients return to daily activities, recreational exercise, and an active lifestyle with greater comfort, mobility, and confidence.

Loss of Strength with Overhead Activity

Failure of Previous Shoulder Procedures

Pain unresponsive to therapy, injections, or bracing.

Benefits of Total Shoulder Replacement Surgery

  • Significantly reduces or eliminates chronic shoulder pain
  • Restores smooth joint movement for daily activities
  • Improves ability to lift, reach, and rotate the arm
  • Corrects joint alignment for more natural motion
  • Designed to restore strength and function through guided rehab
  • Highly durable implant materials for long-term performance
  • Helps reduce inflammation and stiffness around the joint
  • Allows return to work, fitness, and recreational movement
  • Improves sleep quality by relieving night pain
  • Enhances overall comfort and confidence in shoulder use
