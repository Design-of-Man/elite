About Legal Reviews

At ELITE Sports Medicine, our Legal Review services provide an objective, medically accurate evaluation of orthopedic injuries for legal, insurance, and case-related documentation. We carefully review medical records, imaging, prior treatment plans, and functional limitations to determine the true extent of an injury and its impact on daily living or work activity. Our assessments are evidence-based, unbiased, and supported by clear clinical reasoning. Whether part of a personal injury claim, insurance dispute, workers’ compensation case, or pre-surgical liability review — our goal is to ensure that medical findings are accurate, transparent, and defensible , helping attorneys, insurers, and patients make informed, confident decisions.

Benefits of a Comprehensive Legal Review

  • Provides a clear, unbiased medical interpretation of injury findings
  • Ensures medical documentation is thorough and case-ready
  • Helps clarify whether proposed treatments are appropriate and necessary
  • Strengthens legal arguments by supporting claims with objective medical evidence
  • Reduces uncertainty for patients, attorneys, and insurers
  • Identifies inconsistencies or gaps in previous medical evaluations
  • Improves clarity regarding long-term prognosis and recovery timelines
  • Supports accurate determination of functional limitations and work capacity
  • Helps prevent unnecessary, excessive, or premature medical procedures
  • Ensures documentation aligns with accepted medical standards and guidelines
