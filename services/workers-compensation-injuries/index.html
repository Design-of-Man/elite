About Independent Medical Examinations

An Independent Medical Examination is often requested when an objective medical opinion is needed to determine the validity of an injury, the severity of symptoms, and whether current or past treatment is appropriate. At ELITE Sports Medicine , our board-certified physicians conduct thorough, unbiased medical evaluations that include reviewing medical history, imaging, treatment records, and performing a focused physical examination when required. Our approach emphasizes fairness, accuracy, and medical clarity — not advocacy for any party. Each evaluation is carefully documented in a detailed report that outlines diagnosis, functional limitations, treatment recommendations, and prognosis. Whether for disability claims, workers’ compensation, litigation support, or work-related injury assessments, our IMEs help ensure fair case resolution and evidence-based decision-making.
