About the MISHA Minimally Invasive Implantable Shock Absorber

The MISHA™ Knee System is a minimally invasive , joint-preserving implant designed to reduce pain and pressure in the knee for patients with medial compartment osteoarthritis and chronic knee discomfort . Unlike knee replacement surgery, the MISHA implant does not remove bone or cartilage . Instead, it works like a shock absorber, unloading stress from the inner part of the knee during walking and activity.

At Elite Sports Medicine , this system is ideal for patients who experience persistent knee pain that has not responded to conservative treatments such as physical therapy, injections, medications, or bracing, but who are not ready for total knee replacement and wish to stay active. The procedure is performed through a small incision , and recovery is generally faster than traditional joint replacement. Patients typically return to daily movement quickly, with a structured rehabilitation plan focused on improving strength, alignment, and functional mobility.
