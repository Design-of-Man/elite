MISHA Knee System

Schedule a Consultation

What is the MISHA knee implant?

It sits outside the joint and takes load off it. The MISHA™ system is a small shock absorber implanted along the inner side of the knee, which absorbs some of the force that would otherwise pass through the worn medial compartment every time you take a step. Nothing is removed. No bone is cut, no cartilage is taken, and the joint itself is left alone — which is the whole difference between this and a replacement.

It exists for a specific gap: the patient whose inner knee hurts too much to ignore, who has already worked through therapy, injections, medication and bracing, and who is not ready — or not old enough — for a joint replacement. It goes in through a small incision, recovery is quicker than a replacement, and crucially it changes nothing about what you can have done later. If you need a replacement in ten years, that option is still fully open.

Who is it for?

Medial Knee Osteoarthritis

Arthritis confined to the inner compartment of the knee.

Chronic Inner Knee Pain with Activity

Inner-knee pain that builds the longer you are on your feet.

Cartilage Breakdown on the Medial Joint Surface

Cartilage on the inner surface worn thin, so the joint grinds.

Knee Pain After Previous Meniscus Surgery

A knee that has hurt since a meniscectomy took away its cushioning.

Varus (Inward) Knee Alignment Overload

Bow-legged alignment driving extra load through the inner side.

Activity-Limiting Knee Stiffness or Swelling

Stiffness and swelling that have started limiting what you do.

Unresolved Knee Pain After Injections or Therapy

Pain that injections and therapy have not meaningfully touched.

Patients Seeking to Stay Active Without Joint Replacement

Wanting to stay active, and to keep your own joint while doing it.

Why consider it

  • Takes load off the worn inner compartment
  • Eases the pain that shows up when you move
  • Your own bone and cartilage stay where they are
  • A small incision, and a faster recovery than a replacement
  • Back to ordinary activity sooner than replacement allows
  • Nothing is closed off — a replacement remains fully available later
  • Built for people who intend to stay active
  • May slow how fast the arthritis progresses
  • Rehabilitation aimed at mechanics and stability, not just healing
  • Walking, stairs and standing become comfortable again
