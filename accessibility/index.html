Accessibility Statement

Effective date: September 14, 2026

Elite Sports Medicine is committed to making this website usable by everyone, including people who rely on assistive technology. Our goal is to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA.

What we’ve built in

  • A “skip to content” link and consistent landmark structure on every page
  • Full keyboard operability, with visible focus indicators
  • A logical heading hierarchy and descriptive alternative text on images
  • Color contrast that meets WCAG AA on text and interactive elements
  • Animations that respect your device’s “reduce motion” preference
  • Forms with visible labels tied to their fields

Ongoing work

Accessibility is an ongoing effort. We review the site as content changes and welcome feedback.

Need help or found a problem?

If any part of this website is difficult to use, or you would like information from it in another format, please contact us and we will help promptly. You can always reach the practice by phone.
